# Boombat's Missions

A folder of my pre-placed missions for Arma 3 Public Zeus, ranging from my earliest unoptimized pre-placed missions to my newest ones. It is important to note that a lot of compositions made by other people on the Steam Workshop are used in many of these, however I'm uploading all of them here because of popular request. 

## Instructions

Place these in an invisible helipad and save them. The map they are meant for are in the first part of the filename. Some of these missions have garrisoned enemies; those are in the [`garrison`](garrison/) subfolder.

The spawn bases that I am really known for using are in another subfolder, that being the [`mob`](mob/) subfolder.
